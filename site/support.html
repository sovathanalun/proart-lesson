ProArt Support.

Need a hand? Most answers are below. If yours isn't, email sovathanalun@gmail.com with your iPad model, iPadOS version and what happened. We usually reply within two working days.

Purchases

I bought something, but it's still locked

Open the Store or any paywall and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy. A purchase that says "waiting for approval" (Ask to Buy) unlocks once a parent approves it.

What's free and what's in ProArt Pro?

Free: Brush, Smudge and Eraser with starter brushes, ColorDrop fill, Eyedropper, colour wheel, rectangle / ellipse / lasso selection, Transform, crop, resize, rotate and flip, up to 5 layers, PNG and JPEG export, and all free Learn to Draw lessons.

ProArt Pro (one-time purchase): 300+ brushes, Brush Studio and .abr import, Magic Wand, adjustments and Liquify, QuickShape, gradients, text and shapes, retouch tools, unlimited layers, 26 blend modes, groups, masks and alpha lock, Animation Assist, time-lapse video, painting on 3D models, palettes from photos, Pro export formats (HEIC, TIFF, PDF, PSD, layers ZIP, 2× / 4× size) and no ads.

Paid lessons and bundles are sold separately and are not part of Pro.

How do I remove the ads?

Tap Remove ads on the banner for a one-time purchase. Ads also disappear if you buy ProArt Pro or any lesson or bundle. There are never ads while you draw.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the ProArt purchase.

Drawing

Where are my drawings saved?

On your iPad only, and they save automatically. They are not in iCloud, so to keep a copy, use ⋯ → Export… and save to Files or Photos. Deleting the app deletes your drawings.

I can't add more layers

The free version allows 5 layers. With Pro, the limit depends on canvas size and your iPad's memory (up to 200); a smaller canvas allows more layers.

My finger draws instead of scrolling (or the other way round)

Open ⋯ and switch Draw with Finger on or off. With it off, only Apple Pencil draws and your finger moves the canvas.

Undo, redo and gestures

Tap with two fingers to undo and three fingers to redo, or use the arrows on the left sidebar. Pinch to zoom and rotate the canvas.

Lessons

A lesson won't download

Check your internet connection and that there's free space on your iPad, then try again from the Store. Paid lessons are delivered by Apple and can take a moment to start after purchase.

Ads and privacy

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tracking: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → ProArt. In Europe and the UK, Google's consent form appears again if you reinstall the app. Read the full Privacy Policy.
