ProArt for iPad

Privacy Policy.

Effective 4 November 2026

ProArt is a drawing app. Your artwork stays on your iPad. There are no ProArt accounts, and we don't run servers that collect anything about you. The only data that leaves your device goes to Apple (for purchases and updates), to GitHub (to download the free lesson list), and to Google (to show ads, unless you remove them).

Your drawings and photos

  • Everything you draw, every layer, time-lapse recording and imported brush is stored only on your iPad, inside ProArt's own storage. We never upload it.
  • When you pick a photo to trace or use as a reference, ProArt reads only that photo, using Apple's photo picker. It doesn't get access to the rest of your library.
  • ProArt asks for permission to add images to Photos so it can save your exported artwork and videos. It can't read your library with that permission.
  • If you share or export a file, it goes wherever you send it. That's your choice and is handled by iPadOS.

Purchases

ProArt Pro, Remove Ads and paid lessons are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your name, Apple ID, card or address. The app only learns which items you own, so it can unlock them. Paid lessons are delivered by Apple as downloads hosted by Apple.

Lessons and updates

  • To show the lesson store, the app downloads a public list of lessons from GitHub Pages (sovathanalun.github.io). Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic device information when this happens. No account or identifier from ProArt is sent. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • To tell you when a new version is available, the app asks Apple's App Store for ProArt's latest version number.

Advertising

The free version shows a banner ad on the Home, Sketches, Learn and Store screens (never while you draw). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Ads are switched off completely if you buy Remove Ads, ProArt Pro, or any lesson or bundle.

  • Your consent comes first. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks whether ads may use your data before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later in the form.
  • Apple's tracking prompt. ProArt asks permission before Google can use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised with it. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • What Google may collect to show and measure ads: your IP address and approximate location from it, device and app information, the advertising identifier (if you allowed it), ad interactions such as views and taps, and crash and performance data from the ads library.
  • Google is responsible for that data under Google's Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, no ProArt account, no email collection.
  • No analytics of our own, and we don't sell your data.
  • We never access your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Children

ProArt is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. If you're a parent and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll help. In practice we hold none, because nothing is sent to us.

Deleting your data

Delete artworks inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPad. Purchase records stay with your Apple ID; you can use Restore Purchases after reinstalling.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
